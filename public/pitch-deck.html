JanaSetu AI — a multilingual, evidence-aware citizen assistant that helps people understand potential pathways and prepare what comes next.

Understand what you may qualify for. Prepare what comes next.
01 · THE PROBLEM

Finding a scheme is only the first step.

Citizens still need to interpret eligibility language, identify missing evidence, organise documents and move between department channels. A directory of links does not manage that journey.

Fragmented rulesRequirements are spread across scheme pages, notifications and portals.
Evidence frictionPeople struggle to map personal facts and documents to each condition.
Unclear next stepsUnknowns and missing documents can cause avoidable rework.
Trust gapCitizens need clear sources, uncertainty and human control.
02 · THE SOLUTION

One citizen workspace. A traceable path to action.

JanaSetu links a citizen's profile to source-linked scheme conditions, evidence status, missing information and a preparation checklist.

DiscoverExplore a partial, source-aware scheme catalogue.
AssessEvaluate deterministic prototype rules; unknown stays unknown.
PrepareOrganise a draft, evidence checklist and follow-up.
03 · AGENT WORKFLOW

Not just a chatbot transcript.

A stateful workflow coordinates retrieval, deterministic evaluation and preparation while keeping consequential actions under citizen control.

Scheme retrievalFind records and preserve official source references.
Rule evaluationApply explicit conditions and expose matched, not-matched or unknown.
Evidence mappingConnect profile facts to evidence gaps and next actions.
Human reviewPrepare drafts; no automatic government submission.
04 · PRIVACY & TRUST

Local-first document control.

New document binaries are stored in browser IndexedDB. A portable export encrypts workspace data and selected files using AES-256-GCM with a PBKDF2-derived key. Passwords remain in the browser.

Encrypted exportPer-export salt and nonce; incorrect passwords or tampering fail authentication.
Clear boundariesLocal browser storage is not app-level encrypted at rest. Malware scanning is separate.
05 · BUILD & IMPACT

Built as a working prototype. Governed for trust.

Next.js, React, TypeScript, Supabase/Postgres, browser Web Crypto, IndexedDB and ClamAV tooling. CI passes on the submitted branch and a public Vercel preview is available.

Prototype todayCitizen workspace, preliminary screening, preparation flow, encrypted local vault.
Next validationIndependent scheme reviews, legal consent approval, authorised APIs and pilot evaluation.

JanaSetu is not a government service or eligibility authority. No scheme is currently independently approved for production use.
