# Three-minute demonstration script

## Setup
Use synthetic data only. Never enter a real citizen's name, identity number, bank information, portal password, OTP or document.

Synthetic profile:
- Name: Ananya Rao
- Age: 34
- State: Karnataka
- District: Mysuru
- Residence: Urban
- Annual household income: ₹1,50,000
- Occupation: Farmer

## Run of show
**0:00–0:25 — Problem.** “Finding a scheme is only the first step. Citizens still need to translate eligibility rules into evidence, prepare documents and manage next actions. JanaSetu focuses on that preparation gap.”

**0:25–0:55 — Profile.** Open the workspace, enter the synthetic profile and show the six-language selector. Explain that all State/UT profile choices do not imply complete scheme coverage.

**0:55–1:25 — Assessment.** Run the preliminary assessment. Open the execution trace. Explain deterministic rule evaluation and show how missing facts remain unknown rather than guessed.

**1:25–1:55 — Source transparency.** Open a scheme detail and show its source. Explain that catalogue-only records and discovery leads are not published verified schemes.

**1:55–2:30 — Draft.** Prepare a local draft/checklist and show the Not submitted label. Explain that JanaSetu does not claim official eligibility or submission.

**2:30–3:00 — Continuity.** Show a local reminder and JSON export/restore. Close with: “JanaSetu helps citizens prepare to use official services with clearer evidence and next steps. The department remains the decision-maker.”

## Claims to avoid
Do not claim every scheme is covered, official eligibility/approval, application submission, production document scanning, live cloud sync, DPDP certification, government approval, independent penetration testing or unsupported impact metrics.
