Across India, finding a government scheme is only the beginning. Citizens still need to interpret eligibility rules, connect those rules to their circumstances, identify missing evidence, prepare documents and understand what to do next.

JanaSetu AI is an AI and machine-learning themed, multilingual benefits-readiness platform. Its agent-style workflow connects scheme discovery, structured eligibility checks, evidence gaps and citizen-controlled application preparation in one workspace.

A citizen can explore source-linked scheme information, create a profile, and inspect preliminary conditions. The system distinguishes matched, not-matched and unknown facts. It does not guess when information is missing. Each potential pathway must still be checked against the responsible department and current official rules.

The Benefit Readiness Graph is the core innovation. It connects scheme conditions to their sources, citizen-stated facts, evidence status and the next preparation action. This makes the journey inspectable rather than hiding the reasoning inside a chatbot response.

The prototype supports six interface languages. It includes a starter catalogue and four rule-screened scheme prototypes that are still pending independent review. Citizens can prepare a local checklist and draft, manage follow-ups, and export a portable encrypted vault using AES-256-GCM.

Trust boundaries are explicit. A preliminary screen is not an official eligibility decision. A draft is not a submitted application. Cloud document scanning and government integrations are not enabled in this preview. The responsible department remains the final authority.

JanaSetu AI: from fragmented scheme information to clearer, evidence-aware benefit readiness.
