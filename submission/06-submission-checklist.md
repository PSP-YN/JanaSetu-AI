## Judge Q&A

**What problem does JanaSetu solve?**  
It reduces the gap between discovering a public benefit and preparing to act by connecting scheme conditions, citizen facts, evidence gaps and next steps.

**How is it different from a chatbot?**  
It structures a traceable workflow: source retrieval, deterministic condition evaluation, unknown-state handling, evidence gaps, draft preparation and follow-up. The citizen controls consequential actions.

**Does it cover every scheme in India?**  
No. Profile selection supports all States/UTs, but the current catalogue is partial. Four seeded records remain unpublished and pending independent review.

**Does it determine official eligibility?**  
No. It provides preliminary screening only. The competent government department makes the official decision.

**Does it submit applications automatically?**  
No. Draft preparation and limited citizen-reviewed form assistance are not submission. Government integrations require written authorization, a tested scheme-specific adapter and explicit citizen confirmation.

**Is citizen data stored in the cloud?**  
Cloud workspace synchronization and document storage remain disabled pending legal consent approval and operational verification. Local-first use supports a user-controlled JSON backup; that file contains personal data and must be kept private.

**What is the impact model?**  
This is a prototype and pilot proposal. Do not claim adoption, revenue, government partnership or quantified impact without evidence. Evaluate using expert-reviewed cases and citizen usability testing.

**What is next?**  
Complete independent scheme review, legal consent approval, secure account lifecycle, persistent malware scanner deployment, authorized government integrations and measured security/usability/capacity testing.

## Final statement
JanaSetu is an independent public-benefits readiness prototype. It makes the path from scheme information to citizen preparation more understandable, traceable and accessible—without pretending to replace the government authority that decides or receives applications.
