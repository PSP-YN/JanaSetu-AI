# Product overview

## Problem
Finding a government benefit is only the first step. A citizen still has to understand eligibility language, map personal circumstances to each condition, identify missing evidence, prepare information and navigate the correct official channel. Repeating that work across disconnected schemes creates friction and uncertainty.

## Solution
JanaSetu is a multilingual, source-aware benefits-readiness workspace. Its workflow connects a reusable citizen profile to source-linked scheme information, preliminary screening, evidence gaps, draft preparation and follow-up.

1. Capture a citizen's stated circumstances in a structured profile.
2. Discover catalogue entries with source provenance.
3. Evaluate supported conditions deterministically; missing or unsupported facts remain unknown.
4. Explain preliminary outcomes and show official source links.
5. Generate an application-preparation draft and checklist.
6. Track next steps and reminders.
7. Export and restore a versioned JSON backup for user-controlled continuity.

## Intended users
Households exploring central and State/UT benefit pathways; farmers; women and households exploring clean-cooking support; urban households exploring housing programmes; students exploring scholarships; and assisted-service workers.

## Differentiation: Benefit Readiness Graph
JanaSetu connects a versioned scheme condition, its official source and check date, the citizen fact or document needed, a matched/not-matched/unknown state, the missing evidence and the next action. The current prototype implements a limited slice of this traceability. It does not claim full national scheme coverage.

## Current demonstrable scope
- Six interface languages: English, Hindi, Kannada, Tamil, Telugu and Malayalam.
- Profile choices for Indian States and Union Territories.
- Source-linked catalogue records and deterministic preliminary assessment for a small set of Central schemes.
- Unknown/missing information handling and source links.
- Drafts clearly marked Not submitted.
- Local reminders and document-metadata register.
- Versioned JSON export/restore.
- Optional browser extension for citizen-reviewed field assistance on supported HTTPS government hosts; it does not intentionally submit applications.

## Impact thesis and measurement
Expected impact is reduced navigation friction and better preparation—not guaranteed benefit approval. A pilot should measure rule-level precision/recall on expert-reviewed synthetic cases, source coverage, missing-evidence accuracy, end-to-end task completion, citizen comprehension and accessible task completion. No measured accuracy, beneficiary count or time-saved metric is claimed yet.
