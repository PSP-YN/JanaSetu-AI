 # JanaSetu AI — Judge Demonstration Script

**Target duration:** 2 minutes 36 seconds  
**Format:** 16:9 landscape, English voiceover and captions

## 1. Opening — the problem

Namaste, judges. India has many public-benefit schemes, scholarships and essential services. But the citizen journey is fragmented. People must discover the right scheme, understand complex rules, identify documents and navigate different portals. Language and form complexity make this difficult, especially for first-time digital-service users. JanaSetu starts by making that journey easier to understand.

## 2. Introduce JanaSetu

JanaSetu AI is a citizen-controlled bridge from discovering a public service to being prepared to apply. A citizen opens the workspace and describes their situation in their own words. The system suggests profile facts, and the citizen reviews and corrects every suggestion before continuing. For this demonstration, we use typed intake because browser voice transcription depends on the browser's speech service and network.

## 3. Citizen profile

Next, the citizen completes a profile with relevant details such as age, state, district, occupation and income. These details organise a preliminary scheme review. The profile remains editable, and the citizen decides what to provide. JanaSetu is not a government department and does not make official eligibility decisions.

## 4. Find benefits

From Find Benefits, citizens search and filter scheme references, then inspect source links and what still needs verification. Transparency matters: the current catalogue contains 46 source-linked references. Four preliminary rule prototypes are awaiting independent review. A possible match is guidance, not an official eligibility decision; the responsible department remains authoritative.

## 5. Documents

Once a scheme looks relevant, JanaSetu helps organise a document checklist and next steps. Citizens can verify the current document list with the linked official source and keep track of what is missing. This turns a confusing process into a practical preparation task without pretending that an application has already been approved.

## 6. Application preparation and citizen control

In My Applications, preparation drafts and review status stay visible. Citizens can inspect prepared values, identify missing information, download a checklist and open the official application source. Where a compatible browser helper is available, it can preview safe visible fields and fill only values the citizen individually approves. It never clicks Next, Apply, Save or Submit. OTP, CAPTCHA, Aadhaar and e-KYC checks remain with the citizen.

## 7. Close

JanaSetu helps people discover, understand, prepare and review — while the final official action stays in their hands. Clearer access to public services, with the citizen in control.
