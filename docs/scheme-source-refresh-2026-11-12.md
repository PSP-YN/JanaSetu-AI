## PM-KISAN Samman Nidhi

Official portal: https://pmkisan.gov.in/

The portal currently states that the 24th instalment was released on 30 October 2026, eKYC is mandatory for registered farmers, and biometric eKYC is available through CSC VLE, the PM-KISAN mobile application and authorized state-user logins. It also describes temporary holds pending physical verification for suspected exclusion cases, including land acquired after 1 February 2019 and multiple family-member benefit claims.

**Review implications:** keep the temporary-hold notice distinct from a permanent statutory exclusion; reconcile the complete exclusion matrix and current operational guidelines/circulars; do not turn the portal examples into a complete rule set. Citizens must use official KYS/CSC/state channels for status and eKYC.

## PMAY-U 2.0

Official guideline index: https://pmay-urban.gov.in/guideline
September 2024 English scheme guidelines: https://pmay-urban.gov.in/uploads/guidelines/Operational-Guidelines-of-PMAY-U-2.pdf
Angikaar 2026 operational guidelines: https://pmay-urban.gov.in/uploads/Angikaar-2026-Guidelines.pdf
April 2026 AHP SOP (listed by the official index): https://pmay-urban.gov.in/uploads/guidelines/AHP-SOP.pdf

The official index lists the September 2024 scheme guidelines, an April 2026 AHP SOP and Angikaar 2026 guidance. The September 2024 scheme guidelines define four verticals: BLC, AHP, ARH and ISS. They state EWS annual income up to ₹3 lakh, LIG ₹3–6 lakh and MIG ₹6–9 lakh, with State/UT flexibility to redefine EWS with Ministry concurrence. They define the beneficiary family as husband, wife and unmarried sons/daughters; prohibit owning a pucca house anywhere in India; and disallow benefit where a house was allotted under a government housing scheme in the preceding 20 years. State/UT/ULB selection and vertical-specific requirements remain material.

Angikaar 2026 is a last-mile outreach campaign (4 May–31 July 2026), not itself a replacement eligibility rulebook. The April 2026 AHP SOP is an additional current source that must be read before an AHP rule can be approved.

**Review implications:** correct any shorthand that confuses ARH with ISSR; review each of the four verticals independently; obtain and reconcile the AHP SOP and applicable State/UT instructions; verify any subsequent amendments. Do not collect Aadhaar in this prototype even though official downstream processes may require Aadhaar/Aadhaar Virtual ID.

## PMUY 2.0

Official application page: https://www.pmuy.gov.in/Ujjwala2.html
Official FAQ: https://www.pmuy.gov.in/faq.html

The official FAQ states that the beneficiary is an adult woman from a poor household supported by a deprivation declaration, and that the household must not have an existing LPG connection registered in the name of any family member listed in the family-composition document. The official application page lists KYC, identity/address and family-composition evidence, Aadhaar for applicant/adult family members, bank details and a deprivation declaration.

**Review implications:** retain the 18+ woman / deprivation-declaration requirements; evaluate the household connection test against the full family-composition document, not only the applicant; verify current OMC instructions and connection availability. JanaSetu must not collect Aadhaar, bank details, OTPs or credentials; direct citizens to the distributor/official OMC channel for those steps.

### AHP SOP detail review — 12 November 2026

The full 39-page April 2026 AHP SOP was downloaded from the official guideline index and text-reviewed. It describes two AHP delivery models (public-agency projects and private-sector projects), prospective beneficiaries selecting a project through the Unified Web Portal, and application through that portal, a CSC or the concerned ULB/municipality. It reiterates the PMAY-U 2.0 family definition and income bands, requires family-member details in the official process, and states that merely submitting an AHP application creates no right to benefits; competent-authority due diligence and verification remain necessary. It also says a beneficiary or family member already allotted a house under a Central or State/UT urban housing scheme is not eligible for benefits under that AHP project.

**Additional review implications:** AHP must be modeled as a project-specific route, not a generic housing form. Keep the project-selection step, delivery model, competent-authority verification and no-entitlement-on-submission disclaimer explicit. The SOP references Aadhaar details and income/bank evidence for the official workflow; JanaSetu should continue to avoid collecting those sensitive values and hand the citizen off to the official portal/CSC/ULB. The scheme-level 20-year prior-allotment rule and the SOP's AHP-specific prior urban allotment wording must be reconciled by a qualified rule reviewer rather than silently collapsed into one condition.
