## 3. PMUY 2.0

Official eligibility and application page: https://www.pmuy.gov.in/ujjwala2.html  
Official FAQ: https://www.pmuy.gov.in/faq.html

### Observed on 9 November 2026
- Applicant must be a woman aged 18 or older.
- No other OMC LPG connection may exist in the same household.
- Applicant must belong to a poor household and submit the prescribed deprivation declaration.
- The official page lists KYC, applicant Aadhaar, Aadhaar of adult family members listed in the family-composition document, ration card/State family-composition proof, bank details and deprivation declaration.
- Application may be submitted to a distributor or through the linked OMC portals.

### Reviewer actions
- Reconcile the current FAQ and all OMC operational instructions, including biometric Aadhaar authentication and family-composition edge cases.
- Verify current benefits, deposit-free connection terms, refill/subsidy rules and any current funding/connection availability.
- JanaSetu must not collect Aadhaar or bank account details in this prototype; direct citizens to official OMC channels for those steps.
